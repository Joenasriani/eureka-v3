# Source contract

The governing product target comes from the v5.5 proposed source package and remains subordinate to the v5.1 PDF trunk.

The app must preserve experience before abstraction, search for a tension, opportunity, contradiction, success pattern, or transfer gap, extract a relational signature, search P1 through P4 source pools, generate candidate re representations, justify transfer through structural mapping, attack serious candidates, keep evidence, structural status, personal fit, user recognition, and transfer outcome separate, allow null outcomes, capture recognition only from the user, and let real world outcomes update only the scope they support.

The Adaptive Bisociation Leveler is a later product requirement derived from the audit. It may adjust retrieval distance, abstraction, bridge cost, and ordering. It may not change evidence status or structural validity.

The product does not guarantee an AHA. The system produces candidates. The user alone can report recognition.
