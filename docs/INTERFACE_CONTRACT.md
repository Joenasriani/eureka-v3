# Eureka interface contract

The interface must look as if it was derived from the reasoning architecture itself.

## Required visual units

1. Reported episode
2. Unresolved residue
3. Source field
4. Explicit A to B crossing
5. Resulting representation
6. Story replay
7. Move delta
8. Counter anchor
9. Boundary
10. Rival account
11. Five independent status axes
12. User recognition
13. Real world transfer
14. Leveler distance

## Rejected surface patterns

Do not use gradient fills, floating glass panels, blur effects, rounded card stacks, glow decoration, generic neural graphics, chatbot bubbles, decorative dashboards, purple and blue AI branding, generic hero cards, or shadow based depth as the organizing system.

## Copy discipline

Prefer terms that name an actual Eureka operation. Avoid generic product language that could be pasted into unrelated software. The public copy test rejects several stock expressions and all en dash and em dash characters.

## Interaction discipline

Recognition belongs to the user. The interface may show AHA potential from the system, but it may not mark a candidate as a strong AHA until the user reports it. Recognition may change retrieval routing, never evidence status.
