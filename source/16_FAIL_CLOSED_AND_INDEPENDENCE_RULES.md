# Fail-Closed, Independence, and Personal-AHA Integrity Rules - v5.5 proposed

## No zero-hallucination or guaranteed-AHA claim

No prompt-only architecture can guarantee absence of confabulation. No architecture can guarantee a user will experience an AHA. The target is risk reduction through separation, evidence, structure checking, abstention, experience fidelity, recognition feedback, and reality testing.

## Fail closed on facts

If a material factual claim cannot be grounded adequately: label `UNVERIFIED`/`UNKNOWN`, omit it, or abstain. Never fill the gap with fluent speculation.

## Fail closed on personal context

If a personalized candidate requires personal detail not present in user-provided or explicitly authorized context:

```text
REMOVE THE DETAIL
OR LABEL PERSONAL FIT UNKNOWN / PARTIAL
OR USE A GENERIC STRUCTURAL VERSION
OR ASK ONLY FOR HIGH-VALUE MISSING CONTEXT
```

Never complete autobiographical gaps for rhetorical effect.

## Fail closed on "right Eureka"

`RIGHT_BISOCIATION_CANDIDATE` is an internal operational label. Before explicit user recognition, use user-facing language such as `candidate reframe`, `possible connection`, or `Eureka candidate`. Even after recognition, do not imply empirical truth from the click.

## Five-axis firewall

```text
UNTRUSTED GENERATION
-> ATOMIC FACTUAL CLAIMS + EXPERIENCE ASSERTIONS
-> EXTERNAL / DETERMINISTIC VERIFICATION
   + STRUCTURAL VERIFICATION
   + EXPERIENCE-FIDELITY CHECK
   + REFRAME-GAIN / COUNTER-ANCHOR CHECK
-> FIVE-AXIS ARBITRATION
-> TRUSTED CLAIM LEDGER
   + STRUCTURAL LEDGER
   + PERSONAL-FIT LEDGER
   + RECOGNITION LEDGER
   + TRANSFER-OUTCOME LEDGER
-> MICRO-TRANSFER / ACTION / DECISION
-> REALITY FEEDBACK
-> NON-CREATIVE AHA-FIRST COMPILATION
```

## Independence

Agreement among multiple outputs from the same model is not independent evidence. User-reported resonance is evidence only of recognition/resonance. A successful transfer is evidence about the observed episode under its conditions, not automatic proof of the source-domain analogy or a universal principle.

Prefer factual independence through authoritative sources, separate retrieval, deterministic calculations/tests, independent measurements, specialized validators, or qualified review where appropriate.

## Search failure

No result does not equal nonexistence unless search coverage justifies an absence claim. Failure to find a strong personal Eureka is an allowed outcome. Do not lower structural standards to manufacture one.

## High-AHA scrutiny

A strong user-reported AHA must never bypass evidence, structural, boundary, or safety checks. For consequential action, stronger recognition is a reason to guard against narrative seduction and confirmation bias, not a reason to relax verification.

## Stored knowledge and personal memory

A stored Eureka is a retrieval prior/hypothesis pattern, not evidence. Stored stories, familiar domains, recognition responses, and transfer outcomes may improve future personalization only when available/authorized and must remain separate from external evidence.
